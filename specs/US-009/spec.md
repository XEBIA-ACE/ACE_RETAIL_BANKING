# Feature Specification: US-009

## Story
**US-009 – View Smart Notification History List**

As a retail banking customer, I want to view a consolidated history of my smart notifications, so that I can confirm what alerts were generated for my accounts.

---

## Behavior Summary

The notification history screen must display past smart notifications in reverse-chronological order for authenticated customers who have enabled notification types and have past events; show a graceful empty state when no history exists; persist each notification to the in-app history upon delivery completion in accordance with defined retention rules; and load the list within the app-wide performance targets defined for standard screens (NFR-001 to NFR-003).

---

## User Scenarios & Acceptance Criteria

- **US-009-ac-1**: Given an authenticated retail banking customer with at least one enabled smart-notification type and past notification events exist
When the customer opens the notification history screen
Then a list of relevant past smart notifications (e.g., large transaction, low balance, bill reminders) generated for that customer is displayed in reverse-chronological order

- **US-009-ac-2**: Given an authenticated retail banking customer with no past smart notifications
When the customer opens the notification history screen
Then the screen indicates that no notification history is available without showing error messages

- **US-009-ac-3**: Given a smart notification is generated by the Smart Notifications capability for a customer
When delivery processing for that notification completes
Then the notification appears in the customer's in-app notification history in line with the defined retention rules

- **US-009-ac-4**: Given notification history is requested by a customer
When the notification history screen is opened under normal supported load
Then the list content loads within the app-wide performance targets defined for standard screens (per NFR-001 to NFR-003)

---

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001 [US-009-ac-1]**: The notification history screen MUST be accessible only to authenticated retail banking customers. When the screen is opened and the customer has at least one enabled smart-notification type with past notification events, the system MUST retrieve and display the full list of relevant past smart notifications generated for that customer (including but not limited to: large transaction alerts, low balance alerts, and bill reminders). The list MUST be ordered in reverse-chronological order (most recent notification first).

- **FR-002 [US-009-ac-2]**: When an authenticated retail banking customer opens the notification history screen and no past smart notifications exist for that customer, the system MUST display a clear indication that no notification history is available. The empty-state presentation MUST NOT display error messages or error-state UI elements; it is a normal, expected state.

- **FR-003 [US-009-ac-3]**: Upon completion of delivery processing for any smart notification generated by the Smart Notifications capability, the system MUST persist that notification to the customer's in-app notification history. The notification MUST appear in the history in accordance with the defined retention rules. NOTE: The specific retention rules (duration, maximum record count, archival policy) are not defined in the acceptance criteria and represent an open gap that must be resolved before implementation.

- **FR-004 [US-009-ac-4]**: The notification history screen MUST load its list content within the app-wide performance targets defined for standard screens as specified in NFR-001, NFR-002, and NFR-003, when opened under normal supported load conditions. NOTE: The specific numeric thresholds for NFR-001 to NFR-003 are referenced but not reproduced in the acceptance criteria; the implementation MUST conform to those NFR definitions as the authoritative source.
